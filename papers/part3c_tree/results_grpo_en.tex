\section{Training with tree rollouts}\label{sec:res-tree}

Both tree conditions ran with \NUM{seeds_treekev} seeds of 200 steps, everything else as in Part II's \texttt{rlvr},
which is the flat reference (\NUM{seeds_rlvr} seeds). Table~\ref{tab:tree-acc} gives the final policies' accuracies,
Table~\ref{tab:tree-paired} the paired differences, Table~\ref{tab:tree-stats} what happened inside the trees during
training, and Figure~\ref{fig:tree-curves} the training curves. The intervals resample problems and do not include the
variation between seeds.

\paragraph{The judge's choice is close to a random one.} Every tenth step the roots that were not kept are continued
too, so we know what keeping the chosen two gained over keeping all four. Under \texttt{tree\_kev} the leaves of the
two roots \kev{} ranked highest were correct \NUM{auditgain_all_treekev} points more often than the leaves of all four
roots, averaged over the \NUM{auditgain_records_treekev} audited steps. Under \texttt{tree\_random}, whose choice is
random, the same number is \NUM{auditgain_all_treerand}; it measures the noise of the audit. Keeping the best two roots
in hindsight would have gained \NUM{auditbest_all_treekev}. On the leaves of every step, \kev{}'s \emph{on track}
score of the root separates correct from incorrect leaves with an AUROC of \NUM{otauc_all_treekev} pooled over prompts,
but only \NUM{otaucw_all_treekev} within a prompt, which is the comparison that decides which roots are kept
(Table~\ref{tab:tree-stats}). Offline, on the base policy's solutions cut at 128 tokens, the within-problem AUROC was
\NUM{aucw_128_kevtrack}, and keeping the two best-ranked prefixes raised the share of continuations ending correct by
\NUM{gain_128_kevtrack} (\S\ref{sec:res-prefix}). The two measurements are not on the same prefixes: offline on the base policy's
solutions, in training on the policy's own as it improves. Its roots lead to correct answers much more often (a mean
leaf accuracy of \NUM{auditval_all_treekev}\% over the audits, against a mean value of \NUM{ps_valueall_128} for the
base policy's prefixes offline), which leaves less to choose.

\paragraph{Cost.} A tree generates every root, kept or not, and the continuations of the kept ones:
\NUM{gentok_treekev} thousand tokens per step under \texttt{tree\_kev} and \NUM{gentok_treerand} thousand under
\texttt{tree\_random}, against \NUM{gentok_rlvr} thousand for flat sampling (the last 20 steps). Scoring the roots with
\kev{} on the shared backbone is the only extra cost of the judge's choice.
