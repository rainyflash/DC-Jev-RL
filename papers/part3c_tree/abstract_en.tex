% Part III-C, abstract (included by main_en.tex).
Tree rollouts sample a few prefixes per prompt and branch from them, so that the branches share the prefix tokens. A
judge that recognises a promising partial solution could choose where to branch. We test this with Qwen3.5-4B-Base on
MATH. Offline, \kev{}, an autoregressive judge of the same size and the policy's own log-probability all predict
across problems whether a 128-token prefix will end correct (AUROC \NUM{auc_128_logprob}--\NUM{auc_128_artrack}), but
separate one problem's prefixes only weakly (\NUM{aucw_128_kevcorrect}--\NUM{aucw_128_logprob}); keeping the two of
four prefixes \kev{} ranks highest raises the share of correct continuations by \NUM{gainpt_128_kevtrack} points,
the policy's log-probability by \NUM{gainpt_128_logprob}. In GRPO with tree rollouts (four roots of 128 tokens, two of
them continued four times each, 200 steps, three seeds per condition, the third added after the first two had
finished), \kev{}'s choice of roots raised the share of correct leaves by \NUM{auditgain_all_treekev} points
\ci{\NUM{auditgainlo_all_treekev}}{\NUM{auditgainhi_all_treekev}}, a random choice by \NUM{auditgain_all_treerand},
and the final policies are as accurate as with random roots (MATH-500: \NUM{dvtreerand_treekev_math}
\ci{\NUM{dvtreerandlo_treekev_math}}{\NUM{dvtreerandhi_treekev_math}} points). Both tree conditions end above flat GRPO
in the seed mean, but that comes mostly from the \NUM{treedraftruns} tree runs that switched to opening their solutions in
plain text, a switch that marks the best runs of this project whatever their reward and that none of the
\NUM{flatruns} flat runs made; the other tree runs end close to flat GRPO. The trees generated
\NUM{treegensavemin}--\NUM{treegensavemax} fewer tokens than flat sampling of completions of the same lengths. One
seed with \kev{}'s choice came to repeat its solution after the answer, as did one flat seed with no judge at all. A
fixed-threshold pruning rule, prune a prefix when the judge's $p(\text{yes})<0.3$, prunes
\NUM{thrpruned_128_kevtrack} of the prefixes. Code, logs, every training completion and every number in this paper are in the repository.
