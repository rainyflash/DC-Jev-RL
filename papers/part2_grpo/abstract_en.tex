% Part II, abstract (included by main_en.tex).
\kev{} is a judge built as a LoRA adapter and a pointer head on Qwen3.5-4B-Base, so a GRPO policy trained as another
LoRA adapter on the same base can share one copy of the backbone with it. We measure what that saves and what the judge
adds to GRPO on MATH. On the shared backbone the judge's probabilities stay within \NUM{shared_parity_max} of its fp32
reference, and the trainer needs \NUM{mem_saving_load} less GPU memory after loading than with the judge loaded
separately. Offline, on \NUM{js_completions} solutions of the base policy, \kev{} tells correct from incorrect about as
well as an autoregressive judge of the same size (AUROC \NUM{js_auc_all_kevc} against \NUM{js_auc_all_arc}); the same
model reasoning before it answers is better, at about \NUM{ratio_gen_kevbb} times the cost. In GRPO (200 steps, 2--3
seeds per condition), the verifier alone raises MATH-500 accuracy from \NUM{acc_base_math}\% to \NUM{acc_rlvr_math}\%.
With \kev{}'s probability of correctness added to the reward, or the verifier gated by \kev{}'s score, one seed of each
condition ends far ahead (\NUM{acc_kevadd_math_s3} and \NUM{acc_kevgate_math_s2}\% on MATH-500) and the others end near
the verifier-only runs. The two far seeds had switched to writing an informal draft before the formatted solution. Of
the \NUM{allruns} GRPO runs of this project, the \NUM{alldraftruns} that made this switch, some without a judge in their
reward, are the \NUM{alldraftruns} best on AIME24; the verdicts on the training completions do not show what set the
switch off, and with 2--3 seeds we cannot attribute it to the judge. Trained on \kev{}'s score alone, the policy learns
to write solutions that \kev{} rates as correct when they are not, and ends at \NUM{acc_kevonly_math}\% on MATH-500.
\kev{} also pays for solutions that solve the problem again after answering, a style in which \NUM{allkevrepeat} of the
\NUM{allkevruns} runs with \kev{} in the reward ended. Code, logs, every training completion and every number in this
paper are in the repository.
