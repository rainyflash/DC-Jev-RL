\section{What the shared backbone saves}\label{sec:res-shared}

\paragraph{The judge is unchanged.} On \NUM{shared_parity_questions} questions about math solutions (reference
solutions of GSM8K and MATH training problems, as written and with the final answer changed), \kev{} on the shared
backbone differs from \kev{}'s fp32 reference by at most \NUM{shared_parity_max} in probability, \NUM{shared_parity_mean}
on average, and picks the same option in \NUM{shared_parity_argmax} of the questions. A separately loaded, merged \kev{}
with fused kernels differs by at most \NUM{separate_parity_max}. All three tell the correct solutions from the altered
ones equally well (AUROC \NUM{sanity_auc_fp32reference}, \NUM{sanity_auc_sharedbackbone} and
\NUM{sanity_auc_separatebf16}).

\paragraph{Memory.} The saving is one copy of the backbone, \NUM{mem_backbone_copy}\,GB. After loading, the shared
backbone with both adapters and \kev{}'s head takes \NUM{mem_shared_load}\,GB of GPU memory and the two separate models
\NUM{mem_separate_load}\,GB, \NUM{mem_saving_load} less. Over a policy update on a fixed batch (8 sequences of 1024
tokens, gradient checkpointing) the peak is \NUM{mem_shared_train}\,GB against \NUM{mem_separate_train}\,GB,
\NUM{mem_saving_train} less. Each arrangement was measured in a fresh process with the same step
(\file{experiments/validation/shared_check_idle.*.json}). The peak of the subsequent \kev{} scoring depends mostly on
how many tokens the judge processes at once, which is a setting rather than a property of either design, so we do not
compare it.

\paragraph{Speed.} Switching the active adapter from the policy to \kev{} and back takes \NUM{adapter_switch_ms}\,ms. On
an idle GPU, \kev{} on the shared backbone scores \NUM{rps_shared} of these requests per second, against
\NUM{rps_separate} for the separate merged model. Its adapter is applied unmerged, which costs some speed. On the
policy's own solutions, which are longer, the GRPO trainers' arrangement takes \NUM{costreq_kevbb3}\,ms per request of
three questions (Table~\ref{tab:judge-cost}). An earlier measurement of the shared arrangement gave
\NUM{rps_shared_contended} requests per second. It was taken while another process used the GPU and is not a property of
the design.
