\begin{correction}
Versions 1.0--3.0 of this paper are withdrawn. Their results were not produced by the experiments they describe. The
leaderboard values (accuracies, losses, dead-zone and hack rates) were constants in a script, not outputs of training
runs. The memory saving of the shared backbone was not measured: the memory of ``two separate models'' was set to 2.04
times the measured single-backbone memory. This version replaces every result with measured ones. The withdrawn files
are kept in the repository under \texttt{withdrawn/}, and \texttt{CORRECTION.md} lists what was wrong.
\end{correction}

\section*{Reproducibility}
Code, prompts, the learning-rate selection rule and sweep, raw logs (every GRPO step and evaluation, per-problem
evaluation outputs) and the offline studies' completions and judge scores are in the repository
(\texttt{experiments/part2\_llm/}, \texttt{experiments/validation/}). The tables and every number in the text are
generated from them by \texttt{dcjev/analysis/sweep.py}, \texttt{dcjev/analysis/part2.py} and
\texttt{dcjev/analysis/paper\_numbers.py}.
